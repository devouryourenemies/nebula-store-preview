RECONSTITUTION GUIDE
Sanitize: Wipe vial cap with alcohol swab. Let dry 30
seconds.
Draw: Draw desired BAC Water amount into a clean
syringe.
Inject: Inject BAC Water slowly down the side of the vial
— not directly onto the powder.
Dissolve: Gently swirl — do not shake — until fully
dissolved.
Store: Store reconstituted vials refrigerated at 2-8°C.
Use within 28-30 days.
Sterility: Always use a new syringe for each injection to
maintain sterility.

SECTION 3: STORAGE & HANDLING
STORAGE REQUIREMENTS
Lyophilized powder: Refrigerate at 2-8°C or freeze at
-20°C long term.
Reconstituted peptides: Refrigerate at 2-8°C and use
within 28-30 days.
Keep all vials away from direct light and heat.
Never freeze reconstituted vials — this degrades the
complex delicate compound structure.
SHIPPING TO CUSTOMERS
Always include an ice pack inside an insulated mailer.
Use 'Fragile' labeling clearly on all packages.
Ship lyophilized vials — they are far more stable during
transit than reconstituted solutions.
Include a clear notice to refrigerate immediately upon
receipt.
2-3 day shipping maximum recommended for any
reconstituted items.
